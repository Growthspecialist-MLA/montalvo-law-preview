Experience Developed Through Mortgage Litigation

Shaped by the Foreclosure Crisis, Refined Over Decades

During the foreclosure crisis that followed the 2008 financial collapse, Montalvo expanded his practice into intensive consumer mortgage litigation and foreclosure defense.

Armando Montalvo's foreclosure-defense experience includes advanced consumer mortgage litigation training associated with Max Gardner and the Consumer Defense Academy. That training helped shape the firm's detailed, evidence-focused approach to foreclosure defense and mortgage litigation.

How He Works

A Personal Review, Not a Case Number

Financial hardship is stressful enough without also feeling like a case number. Every client gets a personal, in-depth review of their loan, their servicing history, and their goals before any recommendation is made. The firm offers bilingual service in English and Spanish, reflecting the reality of the South Florida community it serves.

Mortgage litigation cases are rarely simple, and the value of nearly five decades of practice shows up less in any single tactic and more in carefully evaluating which path may fit a given situation, and being direct with clients about cost, timeline, and realistic expectations before they commit to anything.
