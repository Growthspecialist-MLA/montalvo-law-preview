Map & Directions

The office is located at 100 Southeast 2nd Street, Suite 2000, Miami, FL 33131, in the heart of Downtown Miami's legal district, a short walk from the Miami-Dade County Courthouse.

Getting here

From I-95 or I-395, take the Downtown Miami exit and head toward Brickell Avenue / SE 2nd Avenue. The building is within the Downtown core, near Flagler Street and Bayfront Park.

Public transit

The Government Center Metrorail and Metromover station is a short walk away, making the office reachable without a car for clients coming from elsewhere in Miami-Dade.

Parking

Metered street parking and public parking garages serve the Downtown Miami area near the building. Call 305-495-4897 ahead of your visit if you'd like specific same-day parking recommendations.

Office hours

Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, or by appointment. Free consultations are available by appointment.
